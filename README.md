# The Printer Finally Answers

Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Printer Finally Answers" (sponsor Mang):
one line of text, written as the office printer's own reply to whoever
jams it, thumps it, and stares at its error codes.

The line:

> You pressed print 14 times — I heard all 14, I am not ignoring you, I am out of cyan.

Why this line: it is the printer's own comeback in the printer's voice —
a counted grievance (14 presses), the everyday trigger (hammering the
print button), and a flat literal machine-logic closer (out of cyan, not
ignoring you). One sentence, one read, no paper-jam cliché.

`main.py` (pure Python 3.13 standard library, no third-party packages)
writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt` and exits 0.
